# Tokenomics

## **JMDT Token Model**

JMDT operates on a **limited-supply ERC-20 token model**, with tokens utilized for **network fees, DID authentication, and ecosystem incentives** (staking and governance were planned items, not live v1 features).

### **Token Distribution**
- 🎯 **20% Pre-mined** (Based on SuperJ app data and pre-mainnet enterprise onboarding)
  - **10%** Reserved for **Jupiter Meta Labs Foundation & R&D**
  - **5%** Incentives for **early adopters and partners**
  - **5%** Listed on **exchanges for liquidity**
- 🎯 **80% of supply** mined **progressively**, ensuring fair distribution
- 🎯 **Token utility** includes **DID authentication and network fees** (staking and governance were planned items, not live features)
